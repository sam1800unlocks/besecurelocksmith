Why rekey after a move

You don't know how many copies are out there.

Keys travel. By the time a house changes hands, copies can be with people you'll never meet.

  • Previous owners…and anyone they ever gave a spare to.
  • Contractors & cleanersEveryone who worked on the house before the sale.
  • Agents & lockboxesShowings mean keys changed hands many times.
  • Neighbors & familyThe one who "kept an eye on the place."

Rekeying changes the pins inside your existing locks. Same hardware, brand-new keys. The old ones stop working the moment we're done.
