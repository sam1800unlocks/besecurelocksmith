5 signs your slider needs attention

Florida humidity, sand and pollen wear down rollers and tracks. Any of these usually means a repair, not a new door.

  • 1Grinds or scrapes as it moves
  • 2Takes two hands to push
  • 3Jumps off the track
  • 4Uneven gap at the top
  • 5Latch won't catch or lock

Sliding door repair FAQ

What does the $97 Track Clean & Tune include?

We clean and lubricate the track, adjust the rollers so the door rides level, and check the latch and handle. If your door needs new rollers, a track repair or a new lock, we show you and give you a price before doing anything else.

Do I need a new sliding door?

Usually not. Most sliding doors that grind, stick or jump the track need new rollers or a cleaned and repaired track, not a new door.

Is there a warranty?

Yes. Our sliding door repairs come with a 1-year warranty.

Do you replace glass or install new doors?

No. We repair rollers, tracks, locks and handles. For broken glass or a full door replacement we'll point you to a glass company.
